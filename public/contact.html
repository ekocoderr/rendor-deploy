Frequently Asked Questions
How long does identity verification take?
Most verifications are completed within 10 minutes automatically. In rare cases requiring manual review, it may take up to 24 hours during peak periods.
What documents are accepted for KYC?
We accept government-issued photo ID (passport, national ID card, or driver's license) plus a proof of address dated within 3 months.
How long do crypto withdrawals take?
Crypto withdrawals are processed instantly once security checks pass. Network confirmation time varies — Bitcoin typically takes 10–60 minutes.
Is my account insured?
Yes. Cold storage assets are covered by a $750M+ insurance policy through Lloyd's of London. We maintain 100% of user funds in cold storage reserves.
What countries are not supported?
Due to regulatory requirements, Crypto.com is currently not available in North Korea, Iran, Cuba, and certain US states. Check our full list in the Help Center.
How do I report unauthorized activity?
Immediately contact our 24/7 live chat and change your password. Our security team will freeze the account and investigate within one hour of your report.
