Our Mission

Crypto.com was founded with a clear mission: to accelerate the world's transition to cryptocurrency. We believe the widespread adoption of cryptocurrency is a net positive for the world — empowering individuals with greater financial freedom.

We're building the infrastructure to support the next generation of financial services. From trading and payments to DeFi and NFTs, Crypto.com is designed to be a complete financial platform for the crypto era.

We're committed to building trusted, secure, and compliant products that you can rely on — no matter where you are in the world.

Our Story

From Monaco to the World

2016
Founded as Monaco
The company was founded in Hong Kong, originally under the name Monaco, with the goal of making crypto accessible to everyday consumers through a Visa card.
2018
Rebranded to Crypto.com
The acquisition of the Crypto.com domain name for $12 million marked a pivotal moment. The company rebranded and expanded its product vision beyond the card.
2019
Exchange & Chain Launch
Launched the Crypto.com Exchange and the Crypto.org Chain (now Cronos), enabling high-throughput DeFi and NFT transactions at near-zero fees.
2021
$700M Arena Naming Rights
Acquired naming rights to the Staples Center in Los Angeles — now Crypto.com Arena — in a landmark $700 million deal. Became a global household name.
2022
100 Million Users
Reached 100 million registered users worldwide, cementing our position as one of the top three crypto platforms by user base.
2024–26
Regulatory Approvals Globally
Obtained licenses and approvals in the EU (MiCA), UAE (VARA), Singapore (MAS), and the United States, expanding compliant operations across all major markets.
