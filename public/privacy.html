Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026  ·  Effective: November 15, 2026

Your privacy matters. This policy explains what personal data we collect, why we collect it, how we use it, and your rights regarding your data under GDPR, CCPA, and applicable privacy laws.

1. Introduction

Foris DAX Global Limited ("Crypto.com," "we," "us," or "our") is committed to protecting the privacy and security of your personal information. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, store, transfer, and protect information about you in connection with your use of our platform, website, mobile applications, and related services (collectively, the "Services").

This policy applies to all users of Crypto.com globally. Where regional laws require specific disclosures or grant additional rights, those are described in the relevant regional appendices referenced below.

2. Information We Collect

Information you provide directly:

  • Account registration data: name, email address, phone number, date of birth
  • Identity verification documents: government-issued ID, passport, driver's license, proof of address
  • Financial information: payment methods, bank account details (for fiat transactions)
  • Communications: messages you send us via support, feedback, or survey responses
  • Profile information: profile photo, preferences, and settings

Information collected automatically:

  • Transaction data: trading history, deposits, withdrawals, and balances
  • Device information: IP address, browser type, operating system, device identifiers
  • Usage data: pages viewed, features used, time spent on platform, click patterns
  • Location data: approximate location derived from IP address; precise location if you grant permission
  • Cookies and similar tracking technologies (see Section 5)

Information from third parties:

  • Identity verification providers (e.g., Jumio, Onfido) for KYC compliance
  • Blockchain analytics providers for AML screening
  • Credit reference agencies for fraud prevention
  • Social media platforms if you choose to connect your account

3. How We Use Your Information

We process your personal data for the following purposes and legal bases:

  • Service provision (Contract): To create and manage your account, process transactions, and provide all features of our platform
  • Legal compliance (Legal obligation): To verify your identity (KYC), prevent money laundering (AML), comply with tax reporting requirements, and respond to lawful government requests
  • Security & fraud prevention (Legitimate interest): To detect and prevent fraud, unauthorized access, and abuse of our platform
  • Customer support (Contract): To respond to your inquiries and resolve issues
  • Product improvement (Legitimate interest): To analyze usage patterns, conduct research, and improve our services
  • Marketing (Consent): To send you promotional communications about our products and services, where you have opted in
  • Personalization (Legitimate interest): To tailor your experience, display relevant content, and provide personalized recommendations

4. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell your personal data to third parties. We may share your information with:

  • Service providers: Companies that help us deliver our services (cloud hosting, payment processors, identity verification, customer support, analytics)
  • Financial institutions: Banks and payment networks required to process fiat transactions
  • Regulatory authorities: As required by law, court order, or regulatory request
  • Law enforcement: When required by applicable law or when we believe disclosure is necessary to protect the rights, property, or safety of Crypto.com, our users, or the public
  • Business transfers: In connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of all or part of our assets, with appropriate confidentiality protections
  • Group companies: Within the Crypto.com group for the purposes described in this policy

All third-party service providers are contractually required to protect your data and use it only for the specified purpose.

5. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

We use cookies and similar technologies (web beacons, pixel tags, local storage) to operate our platform and improve your experience.

Types of cookies we use:

  • Essential cookies: Required for the platform to function. Cannot be disabled.
  • Analytics cookies: Help us understand how users interact with our platform (e.g., Google Analytics)
  • Preference cookies: Remember your settings and preferences
  • Marketing cookies: Used to deliver relevant advertising across the web. Require your consent.

You can manage cookie preferences through our Cookie Settings panel or your browser settings. Note that disabling certain cookies may limit your ability to use some features of our platform.

6. Data Security

We implement comprehensive security measures to protect your personal data, including:

  • AES-256 encryption for data at rest and TLS 1.3 for data in transit
  • Multi-factor authentication requirements for sensitive operations
  • Regular penetration testing and security audits by independent third parties
  • ISO/IEC 27001 and SOC 2 Type II certified security management systems
  • Strict internal access controls — data accessible only on a need-to-know basis
  • 24/7 security operations center monitoring for threats

Despite these measures, no system is entirely secure. If you discover a security vulnerability, please report it responsibly to security@crypto.com.

7. Data Retention

We retain your personal data for as long as necessary to provide our services and comply with legal obligations. Specifically:

  • Account data: retained for the duration of your account plus 7 years following closure (AML/regulatory requirement)
  • Transaction records: retained for minimum 7 years as required by financial regulations
  • Identity documents: retained for 5 years after account closure or the end of our relationship
  • Marketing preferences: until you withdraw consent or unsubscribe
  • Support communications: 3 years from last interaction

8. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Rectification: Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Erasure: Request deletion of your data, subject to legal retention requirements
  • Restriction: Request that we limit processing of your data in certain circumstances
  • Portability: Receive your data in a machine-readable format
  • Objection: Object to processing based on legitimate interests or for direct marketing
  • Withdraw consent: Where processing is based on consent, withdraw it at any time

To exercise your rights, contact our Data Protection Officer at privacy@crypto.com. We will respond within 30 days (or as required by law). We may need to verify your identity before processing your request.

9. International Data Transfers

Crypto.com operates globally, and your data may be transferred to and processed in countries outside your country of residence. For users in the European Economic Area (EEA), UK, and Switzerland, we ensure adequate protection through:

  • EU Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) with all international data processors
  • Adequacy decisions recognized by the European Commission where applicable
  • Binding Corporate Rules (BCRs) for intra-group transfers

10. Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed to individuals under 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If we become aware that we have inadvertently collected data from a person under 18, we will take prompt steps to delete it. If you believe we have collected data from a child, please contact us at privacy@crypto.com.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, technologies, or legal requirements. We will notify you of material changes via email or prominent notice on our platform at least 30 days before the changes take effect. The date of the most recent revision appears at the top of this page.
